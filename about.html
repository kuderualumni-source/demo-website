About NordHaus Renovation

Creating beautiful, functional and comfortable homes in Hamburg.

Your Trusted Renovation Partner in Hamburg

NordHaus Renovation is a professional home renovation company based in Hamburg, specialising in transforming houses and apartments into modern, comfortable living spaces.

Our experienced team provides complete renovation solutions, from initial planning and design ideas to final installation and finishing work.

We believe every home has unique potential. Our goal is to combine quality craftsmanship, modern solutions and customer-focused service to create spaces that our clients love.

Our Story

Founded with a passion for quality construction and interior improvement, NordHaus Renovation started with a simple goal: to provide reliable and professional renovation services for homeowners in Hamburg.

Over the years, we have helped customers improve their kitchens, bathrooms, living areas and complete homes through careful planning and skilled workmanship.
